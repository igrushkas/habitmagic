Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

My Habit Magic ("we", "us", "our") operates the web application at myhabitmagic.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information (via Google OAuth):

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account.
  • A unique user identifier (UID) generated by Firebase Authentication.

User-Generated Content:

  • Goals, outcomes, and action items you create.
  • Completed actions and activity logs.
  • Reflections, daily feedback, and bug reports you submit.
  • Badges, streaks, XP, and other gamification data.
  • Values, gratitude entries, and other personal inputs.

Usage Data:

  • Login timestamps and last active dates.
  • Feature usage patterns (e.g., which modes are used, feedback frequency).
  • Founding member and subscription status.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, a secure cloud database operated by Google. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in accordance with Google Cloud's security standards.

Demo mode data (for users who do not sign in) is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser localStorage to store user preferences and session state. These keys are prefixed with lwp_ and include settings such as:

  • Theme preference (dark/light mode).
  • UI state (collapsed sections, selected filters).
  • First-time experience flags.
  • Demo mode data (if not signed in).

We do not use advertising cookies. We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site (see below).

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage data such as page views, scroll depth, and outbound clicks. This helps us improve the Service. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your IP address, which Google processes in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google Firebase: Authentication (Google OAuth), database (Cloud Firestore), and hosting infrastructure. Subject to Google Firebase Terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for paid subscriptions. We do not store your credit card information. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe in accordance with Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: Static file hosting for the web application.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service.
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Track your goals, habits, and progress within the app.
  • Improve the Service based on feedback and usage patterns.
  • Communicate important updates about the Service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

Demo mode data is stored only on your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Export: Export your data through the app's download/export feature (where available).

To exercise these rights, contact us through the feedback form within the app or at the email address listed on our website.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.
  • Firebase Security Rules to restrict database access to authenticated users and their own data.
  • Google Cloud infrastructure security standards.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by posting a notice within the app or updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the feedback form within the app or at the email address listed on our website.
